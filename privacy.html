Privacy policy

Last updated

BabyReady makes a free emergency-kit builder for families. This policy explains what we collect on this website and what we do with it. In short: your answers stay on your device. We collect only the email address you choose to give us and, if you came from one of our ads, which ad it was.

Your kit answers stay on your device

Who lives in your home, your baby's age and how your baby eats are used only inside your web browser to build your list. They are saved in your browser's local storage so your list is still there if you come back. We never receive them, and we don't send them to Kit, Meta or anyone else. To erase them, choose "Start over" on your list or clear this site's data in your browser.

Which ad you came from

If you reach this site from one of our ads, the link carries three short tags: where the ad ran (for example "meta"), the campaign name (for example "test1") and the ad's name (for example "ad1"). They describe our ad, not you. We save them in your browser's local storage, and if you sign up we send them to Kit with your email address, so we can see which ad brought you. "Start over" erases them from your browser too.

Your email address

When you ask for your full list, you give us your email address. We use it to send you the guide Baby feeding in an emergency, occasional BabyReady emails, and news about the BabyReady app's waitlist. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Our email service, Kit (kit.com), stores your address, the date you signed up and, if you came from an ad, those three ad tags, on our behalf. Kit's own privacy policy is at kit.com/privacy.

Meta pixel

We use the Meta pixel to see how well our Facebook and Instagram ads work. When a page loads, the pixel sends Meta the page address (which can include the ad tags above), your IP address and browser details. When you sign up, it also tells Meta that a sign-up happened, without your email address. Meta may set cookies and link this to your Meta account under Meta's privacy policy. We don't send Meta your email address or any of your kit answers. You can control ad tracking in your Meta ad preferences and in your browser or phone settings.

Other services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web server, they may log your IP address and browser type to deliver the page.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't share your email address except with Kit, which sends our emails.
  • We don't send your kit answers or email address to Meta.
  • This site is meant for parents and caregivers, not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect children's information.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe at any time from any email. You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you (your email address, sign-up date and any ad tags). California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we won't treat you differently for using them. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Not medical advice

The kit list is general preparedness information based on FEMA, CDC, AAP, EPA and FDA guidance. It is not medical advice. Ask your pediatrician about your child's needs and any medicine doses.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
